About me

Currently I work at Apple as a machine learning scientist. I completed my Ph.D. in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Stanford University, where I was co-advised by Prof. Mert Pilanci and Prof. David Tse. Prior to joining Stanford, I obtained my B.S. degree of Computational and Applied Mathematics in School of Mathematical Science at Peking University, advised by Prof. Zaiwen Wen.
